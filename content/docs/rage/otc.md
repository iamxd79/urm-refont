## A reverse Dutch auction

::diagram otc

1. The ecosystem puts a round of RAGE up for sale at fair value.
2. A bonus sits on top, between {{otc.min}} and {{otc.max}}. It grows the longer nobody buys.
3. Wait for a bigger bonus, or buy before someone else does.

When a round sells out, the next drop is announced on X first, by @therageprotocol.

## What you can pay with

HESTIA, ULTRAROUND or $URM. Your tokens are valued at their market price, and you get RAGE at fair value plus the bonus at the moment you trade.
