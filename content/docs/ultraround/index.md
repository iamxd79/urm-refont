## What it is

ULTRAROUND is the ecosystem's first token, launched in 2024. It trades against ETH on Base, and its supply shrinks over time. The burns aren't paid by a tax on your trades. They're paid by the trading fees ULTRAROUND's own liquidity earns.

## Supply

- Launched with a fixed supply of 1,000,000.
- {{circle.burned}} burned so far, which is {{circle.burnedPct}} of the supply.
- No team or advisor allocation.

## Why it's different

| | ULTRAROUND | Typical tax token |
|---|---|---|
| Who pays for burns | Fees the protocol's liquidity earns | You, on every buy and sell |
| Cost to trade | Only the normal Uniswap fee | Uniswap fee plus the tax |
| Burn rate | Adjusts: faster when the price falls | Fixed percentage |

## Buying and selling

Trade it on Uniswap v3 on Base, with ETH or WETH. There's no buy tax and no sell tax. The only cost is the standard pool fee, and that fee is what later funds the burns.

Its liquidity to market cap ratio is {{circle.liqRatio}}, one of the highest for a coin its size.
