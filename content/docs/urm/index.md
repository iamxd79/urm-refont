## What it is

$URM is the ecosystem's stablecoin, pegged to $1 and live on Base. It isn't backed by dollars in a bank. Its peg is defended onchain by the Fortress, a contract that owns $URM's liquidity and steps in on either side when the price drifts.

## What you can do with it

- **Hold it** as a dollar on Base.
- **Earn on it** in [Fountain](/docs/urm/fountain), at a {{fountain.target}} target APY.
- **Borrow it** in the [CDP](/docs/urm/cdp) by locking RAGE, HESTIA or ULTRAROUND.
- **Pay with it** inside the ecosystem, for example in [Rage OTC](/docs/rage/otc).

## How to get it

- Swap USDC for $URM on Uniswap (Base). Check the address on [Contracts](/docs/contracts).
- Use [Earn](/docs/earn) to go from a card payment to $URM in a few taps.
- Mint it against your tokens in the [CDP](/docs/urm/cdp).

## What backs it

The defense is made of USDC held by the Fortress, RAGE, CDP towers, HESTIA and ULTRAROUND. You can see the live split on the [$URM page](/urm).

> **Risk:** a peg defended onchain can still break. In a sharp sell-off the defense can run out of room and $URM can trade under $1 for a while, or for good. Read the [risks](/risks).
