URM stands for **Ultra Round Money**. It's a community-run ecosystem on Base, built around one idea: tokens that make each other stronger instead of fighting for the same liquidity.

## The four engines

| Engine | What it is | Live right now |
|---|---|---|
| [$URM](/docs/urm) | The ecosystem stablecoin. Holds its dollar peg with onchain defense. | Price {{urm.price}} |
| [ULTRAROUND](/docs/ultraround) | A deflationary ETH proxy. Burns supply with pool fees, no tax. | {{circle.burnedPct}} burned |
| [HESTIA](/docs/hestia) | A deflationary store of value paired to USDC. Buyer of last resort. | {{hestia.burnedPct}} burned |
| [RAGE](/docs/rage) | A token backed by a treasury of HESTIA and ULTRAROUND. The demand engine. | Fair value {{rage.fmv}} |

On top of them sit the places you use them: [Fountain](/docs/urm/fountain) to earn on $URM, the [CDP](/docs/urm/cdp) to borrow it, the [Mine and Temple](/docs/mines) to stake, [Rage OTC](/docs/rage/otc) to swap into RAGE with a bonus, and [Earn](/docs/earn) to get from a bank card to all of it.

## Where it came from

An anonymous developer launched ULTRAROUND on Base in 2024. It was built to be deflationary with no transaction tax and no outside yield: its burns are paid for by the trading fees its own liquidity earns. The developer later stepped down and the community took it over.

A few months after launch the project took the name Ultraround Money, after legal pressure from the company behind USDC. Read [Names and tickers](/docs/names) for who's who.

The community then launched HESTIA, RAGE and the $URM stablecoin, each built to cover a weak spot of the others.

## No team allocation

None of the tokens launched with a team or advisor allocation. Marketing and development are paid by the community through the ecosystem multisig.
