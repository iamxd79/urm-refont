## Four taps

1. **Create a passkey wallet.** Face ID or a fingerprint. No seed phrase to write down.
2. **Buy USDC on Base.** With a bank card or Apple Pay. A 0.2% gateway fee shows before you confirm.
3. **Swap to $URM.** One tap. Gas is covered for you.
4. **Deposit in Fountain.** Earn at a {{fountain.target}} target APY. Withdraw anytime.

## Or invest

From the same wallet you can invest USDC in RAGE through the Buying Protocol, with a {{rage.bonus}} bonus on the underlying tokens.

## Then put it to work

- [Fountain](/docs/urm/fountain): earn on $URM.
- [Mine and Temple](/docs/mines): stake HESTIA or ULTRAROUND.
- [CDP](/docs/urm/cdp): borrow $URM against your tokens.
