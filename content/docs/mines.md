## How it works

::diagram mine

1. Deposit HESTIA or ULTRAROUND to hire miners or templars. **Your deposit is locked for good. It can't be withdrawn.**
2. Your crew produces rewards every second, as a share of the contract's balance.
3. Compound your rewards to hire more, or sell them to take profit.

## Why it shouldn't run dry

Classic miner contracts, popular on BNB Chain in 2020 and 2021, left deposits sitting idle. When the balance hit zero, rewards stopped and late joiners lost out.

The Mine and Temple deposit into Peapods Finance instead and hold Peapods pods (pHestia and the ULTRAROUND pod), which earn yield from volatility. More volatility in HESTIA or ULTRAROUND means more yield flowing into the contract. The goal is to keep paying for as long as the tokens trade.

## Selling rules

- Each sale is capped by the sell limit, a share of the contract's balance.
- Every withdrawal pays a 5% tax.
- A miner penalty scales with the size of the sale: up to 1% at the full sell limit, 0.5% at half of it.

Smaller, more frequent sells cost less and keep selling pressure smooth.

> **Risk:** it's still a miner contract. Rewards come from other players and shrink as people sell. If the balance reaches zero, rewards stop. You can get back less than you put in. Read the [risks](/risks).
