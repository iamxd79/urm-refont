## What it is

HESTIA is the ecosystem's second token. It's paired to USDC on Base and built to shrink its supply while building a dollar reserve. It takes ULTRAROUND's idea further and adds a stable reserve that can support the rest of the ecosystem.

## Supply

- Launched with a fixed supply of 1,000,000 on Base.
- {{hestia.burned}} burned so far, which is {{hestia.burnedPct}} of the supply.
- No team or advisor allocation. Fair launch, 100% community funded.

## No tax

The engine runs on fees, which is why HESTIA lives on Base: on mainnet, gas would eat the profit. On Base it stays tiny, so HESTIA has no transaction tax.
