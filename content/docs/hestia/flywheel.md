## The gap

ULTRAROUND's burns are paid by trading fees. When volume drops, fees drop, and burns slow down.

## How HESTIA fills it

HESTIA's engine can send funds to ULTRAROUND's Chaos Engine, or to the ecosystem multisig. That money restarts trading and fees in ULTRAROUND's pool even in quiet markets, so the burns keep going.

## Two burns, one loop

| | ULTRAROUND | HESTIA |
|---|---|---|
| Paired with | ETH | USDC |
| Burns paid by | Trading volume | USDC reserve and liquidity contraction |
| Strongest when | Markets are busy | Prices dip |

1. People trade HESTIA. Fees grow its USDC reserve.
2. HESTIA's engine burns HESTIA and can send extra to ULTRAROUND.
3. ULTRAROUND sees more trading, more fees and more burns.
4. Both grow a shared user base, and the multisig can fund marketing, new products and more burn loops.

## The ecosystem multisig

A community-run multisig can collect USDC from HESTIA's fees and spend it on the ecosystem: listings, campaigns, new products, cross-chain work. In hard markets, HESTIA's dollar reserve can act as a backstop.
